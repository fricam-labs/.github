# Community Code of Conduct

We want Fricam community spaces to be welcoming, useful, and safe for everyone.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, products, and behavior—not people.
- Respect privacy. Do not share credentials, stream URLs, private recordings, personal addresses, or another person's information without permission.
- Keep discussions relevant and avoid spam, harassment, discrimination, or abusive language.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, move, lock, or remove content that does not follow these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in removal from Fricam Labs community spaces.

For a conduct concern, contact the maintainers privately through [the Community repository](https://github.com/fricam-labs/community/security/advisories/new). Do not use public discussions for sensitive reports.
